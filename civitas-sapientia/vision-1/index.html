03 · Urban form · AI scenarios

The master plan: one spine, eight districts

The belt runs east–west along a restored heritage rail line. It is laid out on Feng Shui logic: hills and forest shelter the north, and water and open fields face south. Click a district to explore it, and toggle layers to see the systems underneath.

AI City

A digital twin of every street, pipe, and building runs simulations before anything is built or changed.

AI Society

Ubuntu-style civic councils where residents vote on AI deployments. Every algorithm has a published purpose and an off-switch.

AI Culture

Generative art festivals, human–AI ensembles, and a living museum of computing from the abacus to AGI.

Deployable

Each district ships as a module with a spec, KPIs, and a pilot plan, so any city can copy one district or the whole belt.

05 · Public space · Vitality belt

The Heritage Rail Vitality Belt

A disused rail line becomes a 12 km linear park, modeled on the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park brief. It links the belt east to west, crosses neighborhoods north to south, and stays active 18 hours a day with public space designed for young people.

East–West Integration

One continuous greenway for walking, cycling, and slow autonomous shuttles, with a station plaza every 800 m.

North–South Connectivity

Fourteen green bridges and underpasses stitch the neighborhoods back together across the old rail cut.

Active Public Space

Rail-car cafés, open-air maker labs, robot sports courts, night markets, and outdoor AI demo stages.

Youth-Friendly

Skate and parkour zones, free co-working in old signal boxes, and hack nights with mentors from the labs.

Agora principle: In Athens, the agora was where commerce, debate, and culture met in the open. On the Vitality Belt, labs open their ground floors to the public. Research happens in view, and residents can join it.
06 · Cultural heritage · Urban identity

Three cultures, one identity

The city's character is woven from three threads: railway heritage, the innovation culture of a science district, and an emerging AI culture. Ancient wisdom ties them together.

Thread 1 · Railway heritage

"The line that connected a nation"

Original stations, tracks, and signal equipment are preserved and reused. The Jing-Zhang railway was China's first trunk line designed and built without foreign engineers, and its story of self-reliant engineering becomes the founding myth of the AI belt.

Thread 2 · Innovation culture

"Start here, scale everywhere"

The founder spirit of Zhongguancun: garage-to-global stories, open demo days, and tolerance for failure written into policy, with failed startups getting a second-chance fund.

Thread 3 · AI culture

"Intelligence in service of life"

An annual AI Ma'at Festival, a human–AI orchestra, generative light shows on the rail bridges, and a public "Hall of Algorithms" explaining how the city's AI works.

Wayfinding

Signage that pairs rail typography with circuit-line graphics

Landmark

"The Sapientia Tower," a timber and solar observatory and civic AI center

Ritual

Solstice Night: the whole belt lit by its own renewable surplus

Brand

Flower-of-life sigil meets rail-track geometry

Living systems · Nature + modern

One ecosystem: every system feeds another

Nothing in the city is waste and nothing stands alone. Data centers warm the bathhouses and greenhouses, kitchen scraps rebuild the soil, rain irrigates the farms, and the farms feed the clinics' food-as-medicine programs. Each system pairs an ancient natural method with a modern technology. Click a system to see what it gives and receives.

Urban farming: food grown where people live

Eight farm types, layered from rooftops to old rail tunnels. Each one is run by AI for yield and by communities for culture.

Healing centers: a network, not a building

One flagship campus in Sanctuary Gardens, plus a small healing node in every district. Modern medicine and traditional healing practices work side by side. Licensed clinicians lead care, AI assists, and complementary therapies are offered alongside proven treatment, never in place of it.

Healing journey: a resident's AI health companion notices months of poor sleep → a clinician reviews it and prescribes a plan: forest bathing twice a week, a thermal-bath circuit, a produce box from the Living Villages farm, and a check-in at the neighborhood healing node. The care team tracks progress, and the resident's data stays in the resident's own vault.

Implementation

Three phases to a living AI city

PHASE I · Years 1–3

Seed

  • Ma'at AI Charter adopted; data trust established
  • Digital twin of the full belt
  • Heritage Rail Park, first 4 km opened
  • AV shuttle and robot delivery pilots in the Rail Station Quarter
  • AI clinic and AI tutor pilots in 2 neighborhoods
  • First rooftop farms, a food forest on the rail park, and a pilot healing node
PHASE II · Years 4–7

Grow

  • Full AV transit loop and drone corridors
  • Embodied AI Proving Grounds open
  • Compute campus on a waste-heat district loop
  • All 7 districts active; belt fully connected
  • First annual AI Ma'at Festival
  • Sanctuary Gardens healing campus and thermal baths open; vertical farms on waste heat
PHASE III · Years 8–12

Flourish

  • Net-zero operations across the belt
  • 30% of fresh produce grown in-city; healing node in every district
  • AI services in every neighborhood
  • Blueprint exported to partner cities, domestic and international
  • Seventh-generation review: 150-year stewardship plan
